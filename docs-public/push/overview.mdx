---
title: "Push setup overview"
description: "Configure provider credentials, register a physical device, and verify delivery."
---

Notifie sends mobile push through APNs and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Provider credentials remain
owned by your Apple Developer or Firebase project.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload provider credentials">
    Upload an APNs `.p8` key for Apple delivery or an FCM service-account JSON for Firebase
    delivery. Private material is write-only in the browser and encrypted at rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register a physical device">
    Initialize the SDK, request notification permission, and let the SDK upload the real provider
    token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the setup event">
    Track `notifie_test_notification` from the device. The worker sends a one-time setup
    notification after matching credentials and a device token exist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm presentation">
    Provider acceptance proves handoff, not display. Confirm the notification appears and opens on
    the physical device.
  </Step>
</Steps>
